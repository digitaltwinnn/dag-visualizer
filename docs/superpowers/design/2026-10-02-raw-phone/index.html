The raw snapshot page on phone

Today the phone squeezes the desktop's two columns into one panel: the list on top, the selected snapshot's detail underneath, each with its own scroll. You see seven rows and half a detail pane, and neither has room. The search already opens as a bottom sheet, which works. Four ways to give the list and the detail a full screen each, and one option for the rows.

Frames are a 300×600 phone. Content is illustrative. Nothing here is built; A is today.

Recommend C, with the two-line rows from E. The detail becomes a bottom sheet over the full-height list, which is the gesture the rest of the phone app already uses (Explore, Vitals, Details), so there is nothing new to learn, the selected row stays visible behind the sheet, and swiping down returns to the list. B is the standard mobile pattern and the simplest to build, but it hides the list while you read a snapshot. D keeps everything in one scroll but makes long payloads push the list far away.
