A

One grammar, top to bottom

Every card is the same five layers: hint · controls row (stepper left, the view's one setting right) · scope line · rows · pager. The scope line is the chip, shown in all four whenever a filter is committed. Every row is glyph · name · bar · figure · trailing slot; the bar comes to all four. The trailing slot has one meaning: ✓ = committed, click again releases; chevron = opens only, never commits.

Gains

  • A reader learns one card and knows all four
  • The scope is stated where the list is cut or lensed, with its release beside it
  • The bar makes every explorer a comparison, which the stepper made them for

Costs

  • Snapshots' network rows must stop looking like committing rows (they open only) — they lose the trailing slot
  • Chip + committed-row ✓ say the same thing twice in Hypergraph
B

Scope lives in the card head, not in a chip

Same grammar as A, but the committed scope is stated once, in the card's own head: the title takes a hue-dotted suffix, "Network breakdown · Dor Technologies", and the × to release sits in the head's right corner where every rail card already carries its ×. No chip in the body; the rows keep their ✓.

  • The card head gets longer under a filter; long network names may wrap

C

The scope is the top bar's job; the card only shows its rows

Same row grammar and controls row, but no scope line anywhere: the FILTER control in the top bar already names the committed network and releases it, and a committed row wears its ✓. The History chip goes. Snapshots' network rows still lose the trailing slot so they read as openers.

Gains

  • Quietest card; one place states the filter, the control that sets it
  • Fewest elements to keep consistent

Costs

  • Geography's cut list still says nothing about why it shrank; the reader has to look up at the bar
  • On the phone the top bar's filter word is small and far from the sheet

Common to all three: the Snapshots LIVE control moves into the controls row (stepper left, LIVE right), the History ✓ takes the reserved trailing column, and a network row that only opens never wears a ✓ or a wash.
