The heading row belongs to the level on screen

Snapshots, drilled four levels down. What the row above the list says at each step.

A

Each level has its own measures

The stepper is the current list's column heading. A level with several honest figures gets a stepper of its own; a level with one figure gets a static caps label; a level with no figure gets an empty heading row, hairline only.

B

The stepper is the root's; drilled levels show a fixed figure

Only the top level has a stepper. Drilled levels show their natural figure under a static caps label — SNAPSHOTS, FEE, nothing — and the stepper comes back when the path returns to the root.

My recommendation is A: the heading row is one thing everywhere, "what this list's figure is", and it degrades honestly — a stepper, a label, or nothing — instead of naming a measure the rows below don't carry. Click one and say go.
