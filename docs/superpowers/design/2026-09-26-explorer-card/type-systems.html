What I think is off today, for the record: five different caps-and-tracking labels on one card (eyebrow, stepper, scope chip, depth caption, "SAME SCALE"), bold figures at every depth so a child row shouts as loudly as its parent, and names in dim ink while the numbers beside them are full ink — the eye lands on the number before the name.
