Hover cards — suggested changes

The hover card is the one-line label that follows the pointer over the scene. Three suggestions, each shown as it is today and as it would look; a dashed yellow frame marks what is new. Answer with the numbers you want. Nothing here is built. (Two plain fixes are already in: a node's id is now cut the same way as on its card, and an unlisted snapshot hovers in the neutral grey.)

1Say what the thing is: the card's mark, and no "L0"

Over a global snapshot the hover shows a bare number and an "L0" chip that looks like a layer. Over a metagraph snapshot it shows another bare number. Nothing says which kind of thing the pointer is on. The callouts now wear their card's mark; the hover wears the same one, and the "L0" chip goes.
