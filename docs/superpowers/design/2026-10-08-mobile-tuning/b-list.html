B1 Header plate + row chevron recommended

  • The global header is a plate: an accent-tinted band with an accent hairline under it, "GLOBAL" in accent caps and the number in body mono, age on the right. It reads as the group's head and the rows below as its contents.
  • Every metagraph row ends in a quiet chevron ›, the standard phone sign for "opens a page". The selected row takes its network's colour for the chevron, the edge and the wash.
  • When the global is the selection, its plate deepens to the selection accent and no row carries an edge.
  • Phone only. On desktop the snapshot opens beside the list, so a chevron would promise a page that never comes.
