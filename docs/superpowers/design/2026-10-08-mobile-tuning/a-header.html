A1 The name IS the switcher recommended

  • The middle of the bar is the view's name with its icon, and a pip row under it shows where you are among the four views.
  • Swipe the bar left or right to step to the next or previous view (the name slides; the scene runs its normal transition). Tap the name to open today's view list, for a jump of more than one.
  • The hanging caption under the bar and the word VIEW go: the name says it.
  • ⓘ leaves the bar. About this view and the About page move into the ⚙ sheet together with Source code and Constellation, and the footer row above the dock stands down on phone (36px back for the scene).
