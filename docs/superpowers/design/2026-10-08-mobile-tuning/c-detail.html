C1 Chain position moves to the foot recommended

  • The top of the page keeps the human facts only: what it is, where it anchored, when, what it cost, how big.
  • Height, Sub-height and Blocks are chain references, the same kind of fact as the hashes, so they join the foot plate in the same row style (label left, value right, mono). The plate has a short section label for each group.
  • Result: the plate between facts and tabs is gone, the tabs move up by about 70px, and the page uses two type styles instead of three.
