Coming soon — the drawings, no words

Today the view shows three wireframes, each with a name, an icon and a sentence, in a fixed left-to-right order. That reads as a roadmap: three named features, in this order. The goal here is the opposite claim: more is coming, and nothing says what comes first. Four ways to say that with the drawings alone.

Each frame is the flat view at 16:9: the command bar, the parked node grids in the top band (they stay, as today), and the gallery in the space below. The three drawings are the app's own. Nothing here is built.

Recommend B, the loose table. It is the only one where the layout itself says "unordered": no row to read left to right, no first position, different sizes that rank nothing. The empty dashed frames say "and more" without naming anything. It is also static, so it costs nothing in motion and needs no reduced-motion variant. C says the same thing with a circle but spends permanent motion on a page whose message is "nothing here yet". D is the calmest, but one drawing at a time hides that there are several.
Your call (a) Which layout. (b) Whether the empty dashed frames stay: they are what says "more than these three", but they are also the only made-up element. (c) Whether a drawing keeps its hover tooltip with the feature's name, for anyone who wonders what it is, or stays fully silent.
