Raw snapshot pane — the top section

The pane on the raw page opens with the snapshot's ordinal and five labelled rows before its State / Data / Signers tabs. Today those rows are a plain two-column list at one weight: Timestamp, Fee, Anchored (which actually prints the size), Height · sub and Blocks.

Each option is drawn at the pane's desktop width and at phone width. The values are one real BioFi snapshot. The tabs and what is under them stay as they are in every option. Nothing here is built.
