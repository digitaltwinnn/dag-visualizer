Snapshots — the two callouts

Since today the scene carries two labels when a metagraph snapshot is committed: one on its tile, one on the global snapshot's bar. I checked them at desktop, at tablet width, on dark and on light. They work, and they stay put when another card is opened. Five things are worth changing.

  1. The global snapshot's label lands on the chamber. Its panel stands up and to the right of its ring, which is the middle of the structure, so it covers the ribbons and the other networks' lanes — the part of the scene the label is about.
  2. Its dashed line runs through the brightest part of the scene. Over the lit bars the line disappears on dark, and on light it competes with the ribbon.
  3. Both rings vanish into their own subject. A ring takes the network's colour and sits on a block of that same colour.
  4. The global label repeats the card beside it. "pinned" and the age are already in the Global snapshot card's head. The line that only the scene needs is the last one, "DOR 13 of 16 anchors", because it says what the ring is pointing at.
  5. Nothing ties the pair together in reading order. The chamber reads top to bottom (planes above, global floor below); the labels sit side by side at unrelated heights.

Every drawing below is the real Snapshots frame (1600 × 900, dark, filter All, Dor snapshot 28,673,647 in global snapshot 6,994,941), cropped to the canvas between the rails and shown at true size, so positions and sizes are what you would get. Nothing here is built.
