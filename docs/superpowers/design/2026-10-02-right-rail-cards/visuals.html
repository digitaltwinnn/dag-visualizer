The other cards — where a visual earns its place

The metagraph card's breakdown is now one table whose squares are the bars (D2, built). This page asks which of the other seven cards gain from the same treatment. The short answer: five of them already carry a count that is drawn three different ways or not drawn at all, and all five can use the same table. Two are records and should stay plain.

Nothing here is built. Each pair is the card as it is today on the left and the proposal on the right, at the rail's 346px. Counts are the live ones where I had them; the splits marked illustrative are not measured. The skeleton page is here, the breakdown rounds one and two.

The rule that keeps the rail consistent
One breakdown speciesName, count, mark, in the D2 grid. No dots, no legends, no second bar recipe. A card with one cut drops the first (axis) column because its section label already names the cut.
Square or barA square is always exactly one countable thing (a node, a snapshot). A rate or a size, or a count above 60, is a bar on the same row. Never both in one cut.
Where it sitsOnly in slot 3, under a section label that carries the total. Head, lead, facts, doors and foot stay as built.
ColourA network's rows wear its identity hue; a state wears the status colour; everything else is the neutral ramp.
Recommend Build 1 to 5 as one change: they replace two older breakdown recipes (dot · name · bar · count rows, and inline dots) with the table the metagraph card already uses, so the rail ends with one. Build 6 only as far as the archive reach bar. Leave 7 alone.
Your call (a) Country: the network cut only, or also a second cut by provider (it repeats what the explorer on the left already lists). (b) Node: the archive reach bar, yes or no. (c) Composition: the "lit squares in the whole network" strip, or keep it a plain fact card.
