Metagraph card — the breakdown, five ways

The breakdown answers one question three ways: of this network's online nodes, what do they run, are they ready, and how far back does their archive reach. Each option is drawn twice at the card's 346px: a small network (Dor Technologies, the live figures) and a large one (168 nodes, illustrative split) to show how it scales.

Colour follows the app's existing rule in every option: composition in the network's own hue at stepped strengths, status in the reserved status colours, archive depth in neutral greys. Every part is named in text, so nothing is read from colour alone. Hover a segment for its count. Back to the skeleton page.

Recommend C. It is the only compact option that never truncates, because a sentence wraps where a label column cannot. It draws a bar only where there is a split to show, so the status cut stops being a one-segment bar and says "All 18 ready", and it speaks in the same voice as the lead line above it. On the small network it is about a third shorter than today.
Why not the others as the default B is the tidiest when it fits, and it does not fit: rendered at the card width its labels cut off. D is the most literal and the nicest at 18 nodes, but past about 60 it has to fall back to bars, so the card would change form between networks. E reads fastest and compares worst: three headline numbers lose the second and third parts of each cut.
